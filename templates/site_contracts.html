How it works

Monday to Sunday, then it does the paperwork.

1

Contractors log as they go

Start time, end time, done. Receipts are a photo; the amount is read off the picture and the contractor confirms it. A reminder goes out at 5pm if today is still blank.

2

They press Submit week

One tap tells you the week is final. Until you approve it they can still fix a mistake; after that it's locked.

3

Sunday night, packets build

For every contractor with hours, a PDF packet: timesheet, expenses, the receipt photos, laid out the way your client's AP team expects. A draft invoice lands in Xero with hours, overtime, per diem and expenses as separate lines.

4

You review and approve

Adjust a time, change an expense type, set per diem days, then approve. The PDF and the Xero draft regenerate. Optional client sign-off if a customer wants to approve weeks first.

Questions

What people ask.

When can I use it?

Verde Contracts runs today for one firm: ours. We're building the version that lets other firms sign up with their own branding, admins and Xero connection. Waitlist members get in first, and the first few help shape it.

Do my contractors need to install anything?

No. It's a web app that works in the phone's browser. They get a link, sign in, and can add it to the home screen like an app if they want.

Does it work without Xero?

Yes. The PDF packets build regardless. Xero adds the draft invoice. Other accounting systems are on the list; tell us yours on the form.

What does it cost?

Not set yet. It'll be a flat monthly fee per firm, not per contractor, so growing your bench doesn't grow your bill. Waitlist members get the launch price.
